Scope

Twenty-three rovers were assigned, and this report covers those. Fixed infrastructure is excluded.

"Coverage" means which of our fixed relay sites — the digipeaters and internet gateways (N1GMC-1 and -2, the W1FN sites, and WX1NH-10) — actually heard each rover. For every packet a site passed along, we plot where the rover was when it sent it; the spread of those points shows how far that site could reliably hear, and a hole in the spread is ground it was deaf to.

One capture caveat: the capture pipeline itself went silent for about 11 minutes (15:03–15:14 UTC). Any apparent rover silence in that window is ours, not the tracker's.
